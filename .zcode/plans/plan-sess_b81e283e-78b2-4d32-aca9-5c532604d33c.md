## Plan: Add multi-page navigation with matching Fortune 5 aesthetic

### Stack & conventions (confirmed)
- Next.js **16** App Router (`src/app/`), React 19, Tailwind v4
- Palette: navy `#011331` / `#071f47`, gold `#C59B27` / `#F5D77F`, bg `#F9F8F6`
